# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

ZebroDev aims to maintain an open, respectful, and constructive environment for users, contributors, maintainers, and other participants in the Entask community.

## Expected Behavior

Participants should communicate respectfully, assume good faith while investigating disagreements, focus on the technical or user problem under discussion, and provide actionable feedback. Contributions should be made in a way that supports a welcoming environment for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, disclosure of private information, sexualized conduct in project spaces, and other behavior that makes participation unsafe or unreasonable are not acceptable.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to Entask repositories, Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, project-related communication channels, and other spaces when an individual is representing the project or its community.

## Reporting

To report a conduct concern privately, contact [contact@zebrodev.com](mailto:contact@zebrodev.com) with the subject `Entask Code of Conduct`. Include the relevant context, links, dates, and any information needed for review. Do not use public Issues for sensitive reports.

Reports will be reviewed by the project maintainer. The maintainer may remove content, limit participation, request clarification, or take other reasonable action based on the circumstances. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is not acceptable.
